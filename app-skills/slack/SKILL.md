## Layout
The workspace switcher is the narrow column at the far left (Home, DMs, Activity, Later, More). Next to it is the sidebar listing channels and direct messages. The conversation fills the center with the message composer along its bottom edge; the formatting toolbar and the plus attachment button are inside the composer. The search bar is at the top center of the window. Threads and details open as a right-hand panel. The channel name and member count sit in the header at the top of the conversation.

## Common tasks
- Jump to a channel or person: command-K, then type the name.
- Search messages: click the search bar at the top, or command-G.
- Reply in thread: hover the message, click the speech-bubble icon in the hover toolbar at its right.
- Start a huddle: the headphones button in the header at the top-right of the channel.
- Attach a file: the plus button at the left of the composer, or drag the file in.
- Mark unread / set a reminder: hover the message, click the three dots at the right of the hover toolbar.
- Edit your last message: up-arrow in an empty composer.
- Mute or leave a channel: click the channel name in the header, then the notifications or leave options.

## Pointing hints
- "Where do I type?" — point at the composer along the bottom of the conversation.
- "How do I reply in a thread?" — point at the right end of the message where the hover icons appear.
- "Where is search?" — point at the search bar at the top center.
- "How do I find my DMs?" — point at the DMs icon in the far-left column.
- "How do I start a call?" — point at the headphones (huddle) button at the top-right of the channel header.
- "Where are my saved messages?" — point at Later in the far-left column.

## Gotchas
- Return sends the message by default; shift-return adds a line break (this is a setting under Preferences > Advanced).
- The far-left column differs between workspaces and layouts; Home always returns to the channel list.
- Slack Connect channels and external DMs show a small icon next to the name and may block file uploads.
- In the browser (app.slack.com) some shortcuts are captured by the browser; command-K may need the page focused first.
