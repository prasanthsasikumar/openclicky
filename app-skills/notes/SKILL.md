## Layout
Three columns: folders on the left (iCloud, On My Mac, Smart Folders, Tags at the bottom), the notes list in the middle, and the editor on the right. The toolbar spans the top: the new-note button (pencil in a square) sits above the editor at the left of its section, followed by the format menu (Aa), checklist, table, media/attachment, link, share, and the search field at the far right. Locked notes show a lock in the notes list. Gallery view swaps the middle column for a grid.

## Common tasks
- New note: command-N, or the pencil button in the toolbar.
- New folder: shift-command-N, or the button at the bottom of the left column.
- Checklist: shift-command-L, or the check-circle button in the toolbar.
- Headings and body styles: the Aa button in the toolbar, or shift-command-T for Title, shift-command-H heading, shift-command-B body.
- Table: option-command-T, or the table button in the toolbar.
- Lock a note: File > Lock Note, or the lock inside the share/more menu.
- Search all notes: the search field at the top-right; use the filter for attachments, checklists, shared.
- Pin a note: right-click it in the list, Pin Note; or swipe right on a trackpad.

## Pointing hints
- "How do I make a new note?" — point at the pencil-in-square button in the toolbar above the editor.
- "How do I add a checkbox?" — point at the check-circle button in the toolbar.
- "How do I change the heading?" — point at the Aa button.
- "Where are my folders?" — point at the left column.
- "How do I share this note?" — point at the share button toward the right of the toolbar.
- "Where is search?" — point at the search field at the top-right.

## Gotchas
- Notes stored "On My Mac" do not sync; the account name is the section header in the left column.
- A first line typed in a new note becomes its title automatically.
- Locked notes use the Mac login password or a separate Notes password (Settings > Password); unlocking is per session.
- Collaborators see a person icon on the note in the list; editing may be delayed until iCloud syncs.
