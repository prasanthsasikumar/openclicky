## Layout
The document fills the window. The toolbar along the top has the sidebar button at the far left, zoom buttons, the share button, then the highlight pen, rotate button, and at the right the markup toolbar toggle (a pen tip in a circle) and search. When the markup toolbar is on, a second row appears under the main toolbar with selection, sketch, draw, shapes, text, sign, adjust color, adjust size, and the style controls at the right. The sidebar on the left shows page thumbnails, table of contents, or annotations. Menu bar: File, Edit, View, Go, Tools, Window.

## Common tasks
- Show the markup toolbar: click the pen-tip button at the top-right, or shift-command-A.
- Add text: markup toolbar > the "A" text button, then drag the box into place.
- Sign a PDF: markup toolbar > the signature button (a scribble), create a signature with trackpad or camera, then drag it on.
- Rotate: command-L left, command-R right.
- Crop an image: drag to select, then Tools > Crop, command-K.
- Resize or convert an image: Tools > Adjust Size; File > Export to change the format.
- Merge PDFs: show thumbnails (View > Thumbnails), drag pages from another window into the sidebar.
- Fill a form: click a field and type; Preview detects most form fields automatically.

## Pointing hints
- "How do I annotate this?" — point at the pen-tip button at the top-right of the toolbar.
- "How do I sign?" — point at the signature (scribble) button in the markup row once it is open.
- "How do I add text?" — point at the "A" button in the markup row.
- "Where are the pages?" — point at the thumbnail sidebar on the left; if hidden, point at the sidebar button at the far left of the toolbar.
- "How do I highlight?" — point at the highlighter button in the main toolbar.
- "How do I save as PDF / JPEG?" — mention File > Export and point at the File menu at the top-left of the menu bar.

## Gotchas
- Edits to a PDF autosave into the file; use File > Duplicate first to keep the original.
- Deleting pages needs the thumbnail sidebar and the Delete key on a selected thumbnail.
- Export as PDF flattens annotations; for an image with transparency, choose PNG and tick Alpha.
- Some PDF forms (XFA) show blank; those need Adobe Reader.
